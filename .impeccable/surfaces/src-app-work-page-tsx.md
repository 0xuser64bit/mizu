# /work — Commission the studio

Mode: Persuade. Audience: founders, product leads and teams who arrived from the archive and want to hire the person who made it. Action: start a brief (the Interview signature system), primed by honest price floors. Proof: the archive itself and the page's own build; no client work exists, invent none. Constraints: settled Mizu world (DESIGN.md), no availability claim, no kicker labels, first person singular, reduced-motion and keyboard paths for every interaction. Chosen by Claude on the user's delegation ("you're the boss").

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show the process by doing it: one component is built in front of the visitor, sketch to handoff, as they scroll. Refuses the agency default of three service cards, a pricing table and a contact form.

OWN-WORLD: Mizu's settled world at full commitment. Warm ink layers, paper type, hairline rules, vermilion only for the playhead, selection and action. Archivo wide display, JetBrains Mono for figures, readouts and stage labels, and Instrument Serif italic for one accent word per heading. The stage is a bracketed frame (accent corner brackets).

STORY: The visitor sees a component go from wireframe to surface to states to motion to handed-off code. They believe the studio builds whole systems, not screenshots. They read four commissions with real floors, then brief the maker through the Interview system.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: "Commission the work." at display scale, one first-person line with the $4k floor, a solid "Start a brief" and a ghost "See the rates". Right seven columns: the bracketed stage showing the component as an annotated hairline wireframe with mono leader notes.

FORM: The Build, scroll-driven process specimen. Ranked 4 of 7, dealt as the lead's alternate and locked by Claude on delegation. Seed key 594d803c. Raises: from The Letter, the first-person voice and a signed close; from The Work Order, every commission row pre-fills the brief. Signature interaction: the sticky stage morphs through five data-stage states on one DOM. The playhead is draggable and keyboard-driven (role slider), the states cycle Empty, Loading, Error and Ready, and the readout flips in tabular mono.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
