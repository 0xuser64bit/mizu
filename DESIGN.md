# Mizu interface language

The incumbent package and rendered documentation establish the visual authority.
This expansion preserves it: dark ink (#0f0e0c), raised warm ink (#16130f), paper
(#f4f0e8), vermilion signal (#ff4d1c), and its reversed light surface. Secondary
text must remain readable rather than disappear into the material.

Archivo Variable carries interface text and compact, broad headings. Instrument
Serif is reserved for expressive specimen text. JetBrains Mono handles code,
measurements and small structural labels. Fonts remain optional for consumers.

Hairline rules separate content. The diamond indicates a state or a position;
it is not repeated merely to fill space. Square, precise surfaces and generous
separation contrast with dense, aligned data. Controls have at least 44px targets.

Motion follows cause: selection travels, disclosure unfolds, a changed value
settles, drag tracks the hand. CSS handles transient feedback; Motion handles
interruption and layout. Reduced motion removes travel and continuous movement.
Content remains useful in its resting state.

Documentation is a reading surface. A persistent family index, compact search,
wide live stage and inspectable source make discovery and adoption the central
interaction. Small screens collapse navigation into a native disclosure. Demos
show states and real tasks, not decorative screenshots.

Signature experiences should expose material continuity: comparisons reveal
two versions, reorderable sequences retain identity, transactional actions
communicate actual promise state, and history retains the information changed.
